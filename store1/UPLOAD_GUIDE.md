# Etsy upload guide: Quiet Desk Printables

Approved: all 3 products (2026-11-21). About 5 minutes per listing.
Shop Manager → **Listings** → **+ Add a listing**, then fill the form with the values below.

## Same for all three
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Listing type | **Digital files** |
| Who made it? | **I did** |
| What is it? | **A finished product** |
| When was it made? | **2020–2026** |
| Category | Paper & Party Supplies › Paper › Calendars & Planners (search "planner") |
| Quantity | 999 |
| Renewal | Automatic |
| AI / creativity disclosure | If the form asks how the item was made or whether AI was used, answer **yes, AI-assisted**. Also keep the "AI disclosure" paragraph in the description. |
| Digital files (max 5, 20 MB each) | the two PDFs: `<slug>-US-Letter.pdf` and `<slug>-A4.pdf` |
| Shipping | none (digital) |

## Per listing
Copy **Title**, **Price**, **Tags** and **Description + AI disclosure** from each `listing.md`.
Upload photos **in file-name order**; `01-hero.png` becomes the thumbnail.

| Product | Folder | Photos |
|---|---|---|
| ADHD-friendly planner | `products/adhd-daily-planner/` | 6 |
| Paycheck budget kit | `products/paycheck-budget-kit/` | 7 |
| Small business planner | `products/small-business-planner/` | 5 |

## After publishing
Paste the three listing URLs back to Claude. They are used to track views, favourites and sales against the
60-day target in `RESEARCH.md`.
