## Description
A calm, low-overwhelm printable planner for busy, wandering minds. Every page keeps things simple: big type, generous white space, one soft accent colour and only the boxes you actually need. It is undated, so you can print a page whenever you need it and start again any day.

Who it's for: anyone with ADHD (or an ADHD-ish brain) who finds busy planners overwhelming, students, parents, remote workers, and anyone who wants a gentle way to plan their day.

What's inside (12 pages):
1. Cover: with space for your name and start date
2. Today: Top 3 only, big morning / afternoon / evening time blocks, brain-dump box, done list, water, meds and meal check-ins, energy meter
3. Gentle day: a shrunk-down page for low-energy days (the basics, one small thing, one kind thing, done anyway)
4. This week: Big 3 for the week, Monday to Sunday boxes, "don't forget" box
5. Week in review: how the week felt, wins, what helped, what got in the way, carry forward / let go
6. Habit tracker: 31-day monthly grid with 14 habit rows and a "little wins" box
7. Brain dump: one big box to empty your head, plus Do soon / Later / Ask someone / Let it go
8. One thing at a time: your one thing, first tiny step, four 25-minute focus rings to colour in, break boxes, distraction parking lot
9. Morning routine: write your steps once and tick them off Monday to Sunday, plus a hard-day version
10. Evening routine: same layout for winding down
11. Mood & energy: 31-day tracker with 1–5 circles for mood and energy, a note line and a "patterns I noticed" box
12. Notes: lined page

Sizes: you get two PDFs, US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) and A4 (210 × 297 mm). Both have 12 pages.

How to print: open the PDF in any PDF reader and print at "Actual size" / 100% on regular printer paper. White background and light ink use make it easy on home printers. Print single pages as many times as you like for your own use. Works for writing on tablets in note-taking apps too.
