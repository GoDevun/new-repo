# Store 1 — niche research (Rhea, market research)

Method: desk research of 2026 Etsy best-seller roundups (sources below). These are vendor blogs, so treat them as directional only.
Before scaling any niche, validate it with real search-volume data (eRank or Marmalead free tiers) and by
counting competing listings on Etsy.

## Signals
- High-volume digital categories: printable wall art, SVG cut files, Canva templates, digital planners, wedding stationery.
- Generic planners are crowded. Narrow use-case planners (budget, teacher, wedding, ADHD-friendly) are reported to outperform them.
- Etsy's Oct 2026 AI rules ban AI-generated patterns, recipes and instructions, so those niches are out for us.

## Shortlist (5) and decision
| # | Niche | Why | Risk | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ADHD-friendly daily/weekly planner | Named repeatedly as a strong sub-niche; simple layouts suit code-built PDFs | Crowded; needs a distinct look | **Build now** |
| 2 | Paycheck-to-paycheck budget kit | Budget trackers named as a top use case; evergreen, peaks in January | Very crowded at the generic "budget planner" level | **Build now**, positioned for weekly/biweekly pay |
| 3 | Small-business order & inventory planner | Buyers are Etsy sellers themselves, with a clear practical need | Smaller audience | **Build now** |
| 4 | Teacher planner / classroom printables | Seasonal spikes (July–Sept, January) | Strong incumbents; timing is off for November | Hold until summer |
| 5 | Wedding planning printables | Top-5 category, high price tolerance | Many polished competitors | Test after the first three show data |

## Success metrics (first 60 days)
- Per listing: views → favourites → sales. Kill or rework any listing with fewer than 100 views after 30 days, or no sale after 300 views.
- Shop: net profit ≥ the Claude plan cost per month by day 60. Otherwise pivot once, then stop.
